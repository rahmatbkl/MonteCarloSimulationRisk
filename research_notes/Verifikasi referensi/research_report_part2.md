# Citation and dataset verification, part 2 (report lines 148–250)

Scope: `reports/Monte Carlo pengganti AHP longsor.md`, lines 148–250 (acceptance gates, datasets, recommendations, conclusion, appendix).
Checked on 21 Oct 2026 from the sandbox. All 57 linked URLs were fetched with curl. Where curl was blocked, WebFetch or an authoritative metadata API (Crossref, DataCite, OpenAlex, Europe PMC, PyPI JSON) was used instead.

**Access notes**
- Zenodo returned HTTP 403 ("unusual traffic from your network") to every curl request. Zenodo record metadata was read from DataCite (`api.datacite.org/dois/10.5281/zenodo.<id>`). One record was read with WebFetch.
- ISRIC (`files.isric.org`, `data.isric.org`) failed with "connection reset" or 504.
- `gis.bnpb.go.id` returned HTTP 500 for every service, including the service root ("Could not access any server machines").
- ScienceDirect, ResearchGate, MDPI and `publications.usace.army.mil` returned 403. Springer returned a login redirect. TU Delft returned 503.

Status key:
- **VERIFIED**: I saw the source, or an authoritative record of it, and it supports the claim.
- **CORRECTED**: the citation or claim needs the change shown.
- **CLAIM NOT SUPPORTED**: the source exists but does not say what the report says.
- **NOT FOUND**: the source could not be located.
- **UNVERIFIABLE**: the source could not be reached or read; what I did see is stated.
